# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name

**Fufut Restaurant Management System (FRMS)**

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Overview

## Purpose

The Fufut Restaurant Management System is a web-based platform that digitizes restaurant operations, including table management, order processing, kitchen workflow, payments, inventory, employee management, delivery, and reporting.

The goal is to replace paper-based operations with a centralized system that improves efficiency, reduces mistakes, and provides real-time visibility into business performance.

---

# 2. Business Objectives

* Improve customer service speed
* Reduce order errors
* Track daily sales accurately
* Monitor employee performance
* Manage inventory efficiently
* Support both dine-in and delivery

# 7. Functional Requirements

### Authentication

Users shall log in securely.

Users shall only access permitted modules.

---

### Table Management

System shall display table availability in real time.

System shall prevent assigning occupied tables.

---

### Order Processing

Orders shall instantly appear in the kitchen.

Order modifications shall synchronize automatically.

---

### Kitchen

Chef shall update cooking progress.

Waiters shall receive notifications when food is ready.

---

### Payments

Cashier shall process multiple payment methods.

System shall generate printable receipts.

---

### Inventory

Inventory shall decrease automatically based on recipes (future enhancement).

System shall alert low stock.

---

### Reports

Owner shall view reports by any date range.

Reports shall be exportable to PDF and Excel.

## Recommendation

Since you're building this from scratch, I'd split the work into milestones:

1. **MVP**: Authentication, employee roles, tables, menu, orders, kitchen display, cashier/POS.
2. **Operations**: Inventory, attendance, reservations, reporting.
3. **Advanced**: Delivery, notifications, analytics, exports.
4. **Enterprise**: Multi-branch support, payroll, AI insights, customer loyalty.

This phased approach gets a usable system into the restaurant quickly while leaving room for future expansion.
